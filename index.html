Is this you?

Everyone needs a different version of you. Somewhere along the way, you lost your own.

You run the business or the team reports to you. You're raising children and holding the family together. Your week moves from the boardroom to the school run to your parents and your community, and each one needs a different version of you. When life pulls you in every direction, it is easy to lose your own voice under the noise. You've already invested in coaching, meditation and retreats. They felt good, and then real life took over again.

  • With your kids you think about work. At work you think about your kids.
  • You're a different woman at work, at home and with your parents, and the switching drains you
  • Everyone leans on you, and no one really holds space for you
  • Something is missing and you can't quite name it, and your spiritual life and real life feel like two separate worlds

This is not about becoming a goddess. It is about remembering the one you already are.

Through the nine forms of Durga, we uncover the parts of you that were buried or lost along the way. You don't think your way back. You dance it, talk it through and walk it into your life, one goddess at a time, until your spiritual life and your real life become one life.

How clarity builds

Nine forms of Durga. Twelve sessions. One clearer you.

Twelve 2-hour 1:1 online sessions, ideally a week apart. Each goddess clears another layer of noise. No prior knowledge of Durga needed: you meet her through your body.

  1. Sessions 1–3

    Coming back to yourself

    Shailaputri brings the strength of the mountain. Brahmacharini strips away the roles and expectations. Chandraghanta turns chaos into fuel for clarity.

  2. Sessions 4–6

    Choosing what matters

    Kushmanda helps you seed and call in what you want. Skandamata is love that nurtures and protects, including you. Katyayani gives you the courage to hold boundaries and act.

  3. Sessions 7–9

    Believing in yourself

    Kalaratri dissolves the masks and stories of not being enough. Mahagauri is love that frees you from having to do it all. Siddhidatri brings every part together: whole, and perfect in your imperfection.

  4. Sessions 10–12

    Living it every day

    A shamanic breathwork journey quiets the overthinking mind. Then you weave all nine energies into your own Goddess Dance Mantra, recorded in a closing ceremony.

Before you begin

Questions women ask before saying yes to themselves.

I don't have time for one more thing.
It's one session at a time, ideally a week apart. In my own hardest years, one weekly dance session was what kept me going. This doesn't add to your load; it changes how you carry it.
I've done coaching and meditation. How is this different?
Most of that stays in your head or in a separate box. This is lived in your body and walked into your daily life between every session, so it doesn't fade when real life starts again.
I'm not a dancer. Could I do this?
Yes. No dance background needed. Every movement is shaped to your body, there's nothing to perform, and you stay in control throughout.
Do I need to know anything about Durga?
No. You meet each goddess through movement, story and journaling. Many women begin knowing nothing about her.
Shouldn't I be able to figure this out myself?
You can figure out almost anything. But even leaders need a guide who sees all of them. And 1:1 means this space is yours, not one more place where you hold everyone else.
Who is this not for?
Everyday Goddess is for women who already lead, in a business or a team, and want to lead as their whole selves. If you're hoping it will give you authority you don't yet hold at work, it isn't the right fit, and I'll tell you so honestly on our call.
How do I start?
Book a $35 Connection Call. We'll talk about where you are and where you want to be. If it's a fit, I'll send you a one-page proposal with everything you'd receive.
Is this therapy?
No. Everyday Goddess is a movement and embodiment practice. It can sit beside therapy or medical care, but doesn't replace them.
